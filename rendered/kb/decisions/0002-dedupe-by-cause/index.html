0002 · Duplicate reports share a demonstrated cause

owner mikimik01 / Copilot accepted reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

Date: 30 October 2026

Supersedes: decision 0001. That accepted record is preserved unchanged, not rewritten to match this review.

Context

The review of #4471 separates a shared-parser problem from a booking-v3 date-hint problem. Similar subjects hid different causes; different subjects described the same cause. Browser-derived intake tags also disagreed with the tenant and entry-path evidence.

The old decision’s stated trade-off covered splitting reports of the same bug across different subjects. It did not justify merging unrelated causes under one subject and leaving a problem without its own engineering issue.

Decision

Treat reports as duplicates only when they concern the same product and the evidence supports the same underlying cause. Read the body and resolve the tenant’s locale, booking form, and report channel before deciding.

For the exported sched-prod date reports, reuse tenantOf, causeOf, and sameCause in src/lib/kb. Locale order comes from Intl.DateTimeFormat; intake tags and browser locales are not authoritative. Missing tenant context produces an unknown cause and must not become an automatic match.

Count distinct report IDs, not overlapping export rows. Keep contradictory observations visible and seek more evidence before inventing another cause.

The same tenant can have different causes through the form and app. Reports with different subjects can share a cause. The example deliberately refuses to merge reports from an unknown tenant even if their subjects are identical.

Consequences

Triage requires reading and context lookup instead of a subject-only shortcut. Engineering gets separate issues for independently fixable mechanisms, with counts grounded in the export. A fix for one mechanism cannot close the other without evidence.

The triage page and original incident page now point to this decision and the separate engineering issues. Re-review incoming desk changes instead of treating this export as permanently current.
