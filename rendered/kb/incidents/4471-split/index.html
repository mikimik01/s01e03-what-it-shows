Engineering update: different closure reasons

The course desk update reports that #4471 closed on 5 October 2026, after the export:

  • Parser: FIXED, with fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Its engineering issue is closed as completed.
  • Hint: WON’T FIX, because the booking-v3 form was retired. Its engineering issue is closed as not planned, not as a delivered fix.

This supersedes the earlier snapshot’s statement that a fix was merely queued. The engineering team reported the parser fix; this repository neither implements it nor independently verifies production behavior. Retirement explains the hint issue’s closure without claiming that the hint was corrected. Neither resolution changes how the original reports are counted.

Separate engineering issues

  • Shared parser reads month first. A day-first tenant’s app/email dates, and dates through booking-v2, reach the shared month-first parser. An intended 7 April can become 4 July.
  • Booking-v3 hint disagrees with the form’s parser. For day-first tenants, the form prints MM/DD/YYYY while reading the tenant’s order. Following that hint for 6 August can produce 8 June. Correcting the shared parser does not correct this hint.

Evidence and uncertainty

The desk's one-day observations are unresolved, not proof of a timezone cause. The count footnote shows where the supplied classifier files them. Retain the reports and investigate the mismatch instead of silently excluding them or claiming a confirmed additional bug.

The parser issue explicitly retains them for follow-up under the supplied classification.

Received is not merged

Reports were arriving before July. A later batch of merges records a desk action, not the start of the defect or a sudden burst of newly received reports. Read both series and their dates before making a claim about incident onset.

The rule that replaces subject-only merging

Use the current triage procedure and decision 0002. Confirm the same product and cause before merging. A matching subject does not make the parser and form hint one issue; different subjects do not make reports of the same parser bug different issues. Unknown tenant context is not a match.

Decision 0001 remains unchanged as an accepted historical record. A known ticket status and an old review date do not override the current evidence. Likewise, a closed status alone does not distinguish a fix from a retired feature: retain the desk’s reason for each closure.
